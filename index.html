Who We Are

Driven by a Passion for Better Health

Founded in 2015, Elionyx Health was built on the belief that every person deserves access to high-quality, affordable healthcare. We combine decades of medical expertise with the latest technology to provide truly patient-centred care.

Our integrated care model means your care team stays connected – from your GP to specialist consultants – ensuring you receive cohesive, continuous support throughout your health journey.

  • ✓ Accredited & board-certified medical professionals
  • ✓ State-of-the-art diagnostic technology
